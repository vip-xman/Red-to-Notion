## 📖 详细描述 (中文版)

### Red To Notion

看到小红书上的好内容，一键存进 Notion，不用再复制粘贴。

标题、正文、图片、标签会自动识别，图片按原帖顺序真实上传到 Notion（不是链接，不会失效）。可以保存成普通页面，也可以保存成 Notion 数据库里的一条记录。

**这次更新**
- 图片改为真实上传，不再使用外部链接
- 支持保存到 Notion 数据库
- 保存位置改为树形列表，和你的 Notion 结构一致，一眼找到目标页面
- 修复了多图帖子图片顺序错乱的问题

**怎么用**
1. 安装后点击扩展图标，登录并授权 Notion（登录时勾选允许访问的页面）
2. 打开一篇小红书图文帖，点击扩展图标预览提取出的内容
3. 选择要保存到的页面或数据库
4. 点击保存，完成

**隐私说明**
- 只在你主动点击时读取当前小红书页面的内容
- 通过 Notion 官方 OAuth 登录，不经手你的密码
- 不在自建服务器上存储你的内容，代码开源可审查

## 📖 详细描述 (English)

### Red To Notion

Found something worth keeping on Xiaohongshu? Save it to Notion in one click — no more copy-pasting.

Title, text, images and tags are extracted automatically. Images are uploaded to Notion as real files, in the original order — not links that can break. Save it as a regular page, or as a record in a Notion database.

**What's new**
- Images are now uploaded as real files instead of external links
- Save to a Notion database, not just pages
- The save-location picker is now a tree view matching your real Notion structure
- Fixed image ordering in multi-image posts

**How it works**
1. Click the extension icon, connect your Notion account (choose which pages to allow access to)
2. Open a Xiaohongshu post, click the icon to preview the extracted content
3. Pick a page or database to save to
4. Click save, done

**Privacy**
- Only reads the current Xiaohongshu page when you click the icon
- Uses Notion's official OAuth login — your password is never seen by this extension
- Your content isn't stored on any server we run; the code is open source
